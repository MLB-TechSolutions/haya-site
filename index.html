Your trips, organized from your inbox.

Haya reads the travel booking confirmations in your Gmail (flights, hotels, trains, restaurants, tickets) and turns them into a clear, day-by-day itinerary, with every time shown in the local time zone of where it happens.

Private beta · Android

Trips build themselves

Bookings are grouped into trips automatically, and updated when an airline changes a flight or a booking is cancelled.

Tickets at hand

Boarding passes, PDF tickets and wallet passes stay with their booking and work offline.

Plan the days

See gaps in your day, city guides built from open data, maps and walking routes, and reminders before you need to leave.

How Haya uses your Gmail

Haya asks Google for read-only Gmail access. It cannot send, change or delete email.

It looks at senders and subjects to find likely travel emails and opens only those. Their raw content is deleted within 24 hours; Haya keeps the booking details it extracted and the ticket attachments of those bookings.

Your data is never sold, never used for advertising and never used to train AI models. You can disconnect Gmail or delete all your data from the app at any time.

Haya's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Details are in the privacy policy.
